Privacy Policy — mybox

Last updated: 10 October 2026

mybox is a personal, browser-based tool that lets its owner store their own files in their own Google Drive in encrypted form. It is operated privately by the account owner for personal use. There is no server, no company, and no third-party data collection.

What data the app accesses

With your permission, mybox uses the Google Drive API scope drive.file. This scope grants access only to the files the app itself creates in your Google Drive. mybox cannot see, read, or access any other files in your Drive.

How your data is handled

  • All files are encrypted in your browser before being uploaded. Google Drive stores only encrypted data with encrypted names.
  • The encryption key is derived from a secret phrase that exists only in your browser's memory and is never transmitted or stored by the app.
  • mybox has no backend server. Your data travels only between your browser and your own Google Drive.
  • The app does not collect analytics, does not use advertising, and does not share any data with third parties.

Google user data

mybox requests access to Google Drive solely to upload, list, open, and manage the files you choose to store with it. Access tokens are used only to communicate with Google's APIs on your behalf and are kept encrypted. mybox's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app does not transfer or sell Google user data.

Data retention and deletion

Your files live in your own Google Drive and remain entirely under your control. You can delete them at any time from within the app or directly in Google Drive. You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

For any questions about this policy, contact the account owner at the support email listed on the app's Google consent screen.
